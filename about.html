What is this

Soundyard is where kit from our sound system goes when it gets upgraded, replaced or isn't needed any more. All of it has been used at real events and kept in working order.

Soundyard is run by Psion Systems, a sound, light and experience design studio in London. If you need a rig designed, installed or run rather than bought, that's them.

Condition

Everything here is used. Each listing describes its condition honestly. If you want more photos or details, just ask.

Buying

Hit Buy with PayPal to pay, and we'll get in touch to arrange delivery or collection. Items marked P.O.A. (price on application) are by enquiry only.

Collection & delivery

Collection from Hackney Wick, London. We can deliver too: ask us for a quote and we'll work it out per item.
